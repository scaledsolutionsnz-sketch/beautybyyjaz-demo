What we collect

When you book with Beauty By Jaz we collect your name, contact details and any service notes you share with us, such as allergies or previous lash history. This website itself does not use contact forms and does not store personal information.

How we use it

  • To confirm, prepare for and follow up on your appointments.
  • To keep a record of your lash and brow history so repeat visits match.
  • To let you know about appointment changes.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your details with third parties for marketing. Photos of your lashes or brows are only shared on our social media with your permission.

Third-party services

This website embeds a Google Map and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may set their own cookies subject to Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you at any time.
