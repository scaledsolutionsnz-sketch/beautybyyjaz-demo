Lashes, brows and fresh finishes from Beauty By Jaz.

31 Te Heuheu Street

A quiet studio in the middle of town.

Beauty By Jaz sits on Te Heuheu Street, a short walk from the lakefront. You get the full appointment to yourself: no queue of chairs, no rushing you out the door.

Lashes and brows are close, careful work. The studio keeps things simple so the attention stays where it belongs, on the set you leave with.

  • One client at a time, by appointment
  • Open late Wednesday and Thursday evenings
  • Clear pricing, listed before you book
J

Jaz

Owner and artist. Lash extensions, lifts, brow work and facial waxing, all done in-studio by Jaz herself.

One artist, start to finish.

When you book with Beauty By Jaz, Jaz is the one who answers the phone, does your consult and builds your set. The same hands every visit means your infills match your last appointment, and your lash history never gets lost between staff.
