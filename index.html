FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about lashes, brows, waxing and tooth gems with Jaz in Taupō.

How do I book with Jaz?

Tap Book on Fresha, pick your service and select Jaz as your professional. Her services are listed on the Fresha page for Cosmetics and Aesthetics at 31 Te Heuheu Street. Not sure what to book? The 30 minute Lash Consultation is free. You can also call 027 249 1312.

How long does a full set of lash extensions take?

Allow 1 hr 30 min. Every full set, from Classics ($120) to Medium Volume Wispy ($155), is booked for 1 hr 30 min. You can add a bottom lash tint for $10.

When should I book an infill?

Infills are recommended every 2 to 3 weeks. Book the 1 Week Infill ($50) within 7 days, the 2 Week Infill ($80) within 14 days or the 3 Week Infill ($90) within 21 days. After 21 days you may need a full set.

Can Jaz remove lash extensions from another salon?

Yes. Lash Removal (New Client) is $60, or $50 as Lash Removal Before Redo (New Client) when you book a new full set straight after. Removing another artist’s work can take extra time, depending on the adhesive used. Existing Beauty byy Jaz clients pay $50, or $40 before a redo.

How long do lash lifts and brow lamination last?

Typically 6 to 8 weeks, depending on your natural hair cycle. The Yumi Keratin Lash Lift And Tint is $130 and Brow Lamination is $100. The Ultimate Lift Bundle ($200) combines a lash lift and tint with brow lamination and a brow shape and wax.

How should I arrive for my appointment?

With clean lashes and brows, free from mascara, makeup, oils and skincare products around the eyes. It gives lifts, lamination and infills the best result.

What are Weekend Lashes?

Lightweight cluster lashes for a weekend, event, holiday or special occasion, in Natural or Medium ($40, 45 min). They aren’t lash extensions and are designed for temporary wear.

What facial waxing is on the menu?

Upper lip, chin or sides of face ($20 each), upper lip and chin ($35) and nasal ($10). The Full Face Waxing Refresh ($80) covers a brow shape and tidy, upper lip, chin and sides of face, or it’s $100 with a brow tint added.

How much are tooth gems?

From $49 for 1 gem up to $169 for 4 gems, and custom designs from $79 (price varies). If a gem falls off before 2 months, Gem Reapplication is $38.
