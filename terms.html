Bookings

Appointments at Beauty By Jaz are secured by phone or email. Please arrive on time; clients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to rebook and may be charged for the reserved time.

Cancellations

We ask for at least 24 hours notice to change or cancel an appointment. Missed appointments or cancellations inside 24 hours may incur a fee of up to the full service price.

Services and pricing

Prices shown on this website are a guide and may change. The price confirmed at booking is the price you pay. Infill pricing assumes reasonable retention from your previous set.

Patch tests and suitability

  • Tinting and lifting products may require a patch test before your first appointment.
  • Please let us know about allergies, sensitivities or recent eye treatments when booking.
  • We may decline or adapt a service where it is not safe or suitable on the day.

Payment

Payment is due at the end of your appointment. We accept cash and card.
