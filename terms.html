Bookings

Appointments are booked online through Fresha. Jaz’s services are listed on the Fresha page for Cosmetics and Aesthetics at 31 Te Heuheu Street, Taupō: pick your service, then select Jaz as your professional. You can also call 027 249 1312.

Prices

Prices on this website are in NZ dollars and come from Jaz’s Fresha menu. Fresha shows the current price of each service when you book.

Before your appointment

Please arrive with clean lashes and brows, free from mascara, makeup, oils and skincare products around the eyes.

Infills

Infills are booked by the time since your last lash appointment: within 7 days (1 Week Infill), within 14 days (2 Week Infill) or within 21 days (3 Week Infill). Appointments booked after 21 days may need a full set.

Tooth gems

Gem Reapplication ($38) is only for a gem that falls off before 2 months.

Cancellations, deposits and patch tests

Jaz’s policy on cancellations, deposits and patch tests will be added here once confirmed. If you have a question, please call 027 249 1312 before you book.
